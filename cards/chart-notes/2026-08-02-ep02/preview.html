📊 3-Min Stock Chart Notes EP.02 — Moving averages & the golden cross

Those smooth curves laid over the candles are moving averages.
They are just the average of the last few closes — so why does everyone watch the moment two of them meet?

And a golden cross doesn't mean "it will go up". An average is built from prices that already happened, so a crossing confirms a trend rather than predicting one.
A real one: on July 1, 2025 the S&P 500's 50-day average crossed above its 200-day by just 10 points — and it has stayed above ever since.
