A dollar-cost-averaging backtest where only the way of buying changes. From 2013-10-17, at every trading day close, one side fixes the amount at $1 and the other fixes the quantity at 1 share. The start date is the day 1 share of TQQQ cost $1, so both spend the same money on day one.
Fixing the amount buys more shares when the price is low, so the average cost becomes the harmonic mean of the closes ($4.76); fixing the quantity gives the arithmetic mean ($16.58). The harmonic mean is always less than or equal to the arithmetic mean, so fixed-amount buying always ends with the lower average cost. What TQQQ decides is not which side wins but how wide the gap gets.
