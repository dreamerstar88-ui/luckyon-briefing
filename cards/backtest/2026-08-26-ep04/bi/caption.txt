A 3x ETF moves three times as much in a day. So $3 a day into QQQ, $1.5 into QLD and $1 into TQQQ all move the same dollar amount each day.
That holds for one day only. Stack the days and it does not come out 3x. This run measures how far apart it ends up over 16.5 years.
