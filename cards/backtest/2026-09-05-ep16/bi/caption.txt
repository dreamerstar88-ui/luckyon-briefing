지수는 기준일 2000-01-03을 1배로 놓은 왼쪽 로그축(배당 제외), 금리는 오른쪽 % 축입니다. 오른쪽 축은 0%에서 2%포인트 단위로 고정했습니다.

People say stocks fall when rates rise. Counting 2000 to 2025, 10 of the 13 years the 10-year yield rose, Nasdaq rose too.

The 10Y yield ranged 0.50% to 6.78% and sits at 4.67% now. Over the same span Nasdaq went 6.42x and S&P 500 5.31x.
Indexes are rebased to 1x at 2000-01-03 on a left log axis, price only (no dividends); rates are in percent on a right axis pinned to 0% in 2-point steps.
Fed funds target is the upper bound of the Fed's target range — the number the news quotes.

Before zero rates 2000-01~2008-12: 10Y 6.55%→2.36% · Nasdaq 0.38x · S&P 500 0.63x
Zero rates 2008-12~2022-03: 10Y 2.36%→2.19% · Nasdaq 8.45x · S&P 500 4.77x
Rates rose again 2022-03~2026-08: 10Y 2.19%→4.67% · Nasdaq 1.98x · S&P 500 1.77x

Each year is measured from its first to its last trading day. Of the 13 years the yield fell, Nasdaq fell in 4. 2026 is still running and is not counted.
Period boundaries are Fed policy turns — the start of zero rates and the start of hikes.
