NVIDIA reported FY2027 Q2 results on August 26, 2026. Revenue of $96.2 billion more than doubled from a year ago. But the thing the company spent the most time on during the call was not the growth rate.

"We expect to grow revenue by approximately 70% in fiscal 2028. This is a supply-constrained outlook." — Colette Kress, CFO, in the opening of her prepared remarks. It is the first time NVIDIA has guided a full year ahead.

These 10 cards follow the order the company used on the call.

02 Results — Revenue $96,221M, gross margin 75.0%, operating income $63,734M, diluted EPS $2.46. All GAAP.
03 Hyperscale — AWS is deploying 2 million more GPUs from this quarter through Q2 of fiscal 2029. Cloud industry backlog is over $2 trillion.
04 ACIE — AI Clouds, Industrial & Enterprise: $40.3B, up 138% year over year. Jensen Huang called it "about half of our business" and "growing 100% a year."
05 Full stack — Per gigawatt of data centre built, the NVIDIA share went from $18B with Hopper to $40B with Vera Rubin, because more of the rack is now NVIDIA.
06 Frontier AI labs — ~$50B invested directly, $500B+ raised with six infrastructure capital providers. Kress raised the objection herself: "we know some will call this circular financing."
07 Balance sheet — A record $26.0B returned. Inventory $25.8B to $31.6B, supply and capacity commitments $119B to $279B.
08 Margin — Guided down from 75.0% to 74.0% in Q3 and a 71-72% trough in Q4, on memory pricing.
09 Next year — Customer forecasts point to growth doubling; the company guided ~70% because it is supply-constrained.

Every figure and quote comes only from the SEC 8-K earnings materials (EX-99.1 and EX-99.2) and the FY2027 Q2 earnings call. No analyst estimates or price targets.

The final card is a subjective grouping. Nothing here is investment advice, and any investment decision and its outcome rest with the investor.
